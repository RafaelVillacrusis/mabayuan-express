📋 Terms & Conditions

1. Acceptance of Terms

By registering and using Mabayuan Express, you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms and Conditions. If you do not agree, please do not register.

2. Accuracy of Information

You agree to provide accurate, current, and complete personal information during registration. Submitting false, misleading, or incomplete information may result in:

  • Rejection of your document requests
  • Suspension or termination of your account
  • Referral to appropriate barangay authorities

3. Valid ID Requirement

You must upload a valid government-issued ID during registration. Accepted IDs include: PhilSys ID, Passport, Driver's License, SSS/GSIS ID, Voter's ID, and PRC ID. The ID must be clear, readable, and currently valid.

4. Proof of Residency & Account Approval

You must upload a Barangay Certificate of Residency issued by Barangay Mabayuan. Your account is subject to approval by barangay staff, who will verify your valid ID and certificate. You will receive an email once your account is approved. You will not be able to log in or submit requests until your account has been reviewed and approved. Approval is not guaranteed and is at the discretion of the barangay.

5. Document Requests

  • You may only request documents for yourself unless authorized otherwise.
  • Processing times and fees are determined by the barangay and may change without notice.
  • Requests may be declined if submitted information does not match barangay records.
  • Approved documents must be claimed within the designated period.

6. Privacy & Data Use

Your personal information is collected solely for the purpose of processing barangay document requests in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Your data will not be sold or shared with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.

7. Account Security

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials. You agree to notify the barangay office immediately of any unauthorized use of your account.

8. Prohibited Activities

  • Creating multiple accounts for the same person
  • Attempting to access another resident's account or data
  • Submitting fraudulent documents or information
  • Using the system for any unlawful purpose

9. Modifications

Barangay Mabayuan reserves the right to modify these terms at any time. Continued use of the system after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

10. Contact

For questions or concerns, please visit the Barangay Mabayuan Hall or contact the barangay secretary during office hours.
