Roles & Permissions

What each role can do in Mabayuan Express.

System Admin Highest

Runs and maintains the platform.

  • ✓Manage all accounts, including admins
  • ✓Configure document types
  • ✓Full audit trail & AI usage
  • ✓Export system backup
  • ✗Approve or decline requests

Captain Elevated

Leads the barangay office.

  • ✓Approve or decline requests
  • ✓Configure document types
  • ◐Manage Staff & Captain accounts
  • ✓Announcements, analytics, audit trail
  • ✗Manage System Admins or backups

Staff Standard

Day-to-day processing.

  • ✓Verify resident registrations
  • ✓Set release dates & mark claimed
  • ✓Print documents & reports
  • ✗Approve or decline requests
  • ✗Manage accounts or documents

Resident Limited

Registers on the public site.

  • ✓Request documents with a valid ID
  • ✓Track their own requests
  • ✓Read announcements
  • ✗See other residents' data
  • ✗Open any personnel page

Access Rules

  • Every page and API checks the user's role — anyone without access gets 403 Forbidden.
  • Captains can't create, change or delete System Admin accounts, and can't change or delete their own account.
  • New passwords need at least 8 characters with a letter and a number.
  • Every account change is recorded in the Audit Trail with who made it and when.
  • Residents use a separate login and can never open personnel pages.
